Why we exist

A mother’s care, carried forward.

Rebecca Faramade Abiola spent her life protecting and caring for mothers and children. Her children founded RFAF to preserve that legacy and to bring it to communities where a safe pregnancy and a healthy childhood are still out of reach.

In rural and low-income communities, too many mothers and babies still die from preventable causes: poor nutrition, lack of medical care and inadequate health facilities. We work to change that, one community at a time.

About RFAF

A legacy of care for mothers and children.

RFAF is committed to continuing improvements in the maternal and child health of underserved communities. We also empower women and families with the tools to reach lasting good health for everyone in the family.

Our story

Named for the woman who inspired it.

The foundation preserves the legacy of Rebecca Faramade Abiola, the mother of its founders. Her first concern was always the health of mothers and children, and she spent her life protecting and caring for them.

We established the foundation on 20 August 2025. On the same day we presented a fridge-freezer for vaccine storage to the Primary Health Care Centre in Ago-Are, Atisbo Local Government Area, Oyo State. That is where our work begins.

Our mission

To improve maternal and child health outcomes by providing sustainable, accessible, affordable and quality healthcare services in underserved communities, especially for low-income families.

Our vision

Free or low-cost, quality maternal and child healthcare within reach of every family in remote and underprivileged communities.

Our objectives

What we set out to do

  • Improve maternal and child health outcomes by providing accessible, affordable, quality healthcare in underserved communities.
  • Reduce maternal and infant deaths by tackling preventable causes such as poor nutrition, lack of medical care and inadequate facilities.
  • Improve access to healthcare for pregnant women, mothers and children, especially in rural and low-income areas.
  • Empower women and families through education, resources and support systems that promote safe pregnancies and child well-being.
  • Work with local governments, healthcare institutions and NGOs to strengthen maternal and child healthcare systems.

Projects & impact

What we have done so far.

We are a young foundation, and we report our work as it is. Here is our first completed project and the milestones behind it.

Completed · in use

Vaccine storage for Ago-Are Primary Health Care Centre

On 20 August 2025 we presented a fridge-freezer for vaccine storage to the Primary Health Care Centre in Ago-Are, for use by Atisbo Local Government. It now stores vaccines for mothers and children across the local government area.

Date
20 Aug 2025
Location
Ago-Are, Atisbo LGA, Oyo State
Population served
About 171,800 people
Status
In use for vaccine storage

Why a fridge matters

Most vaccines must stay between +2 °C and +8 °C from the factory to the moment they are given. Outside that range they can lose their strength. A dependable fridge at the health centre keeps vaccines safe close to the families who need them.

0 °C safe range +2 °C +8 °C
